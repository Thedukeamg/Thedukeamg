# Derrick Aryee

### Building practical software for businesses—and a little fun along the way.

I'm building products across business finance, everyday workflows, tax planning and games. This profile is a window into that work.

## Selected projects

### VaydaOS · Business funding operations
A CRM built around the work behind business funding: managing leads, collecting applications, reviewing documents, coordinating lender submissions and tracking outcomes. It also brings calling tools and staff workflows into one workspace.

**Focus:** clear workflows, organized information and less repetitive administrative work.

### TaskNoll · Business workflows
A business-workflow product in development, with a private phone pilot. I'm building and testing the experience before sharing more of the implementation publicly.

**Focus:** making everyday work easier to manage.

### TaxVayda · Proactive tax planning
A tax-planning app exploring educational forecasts, scenario comparisons and potential tax-saving opportunities before year-end. Built with Expo, React Native, TypeScript and Supabase.

**Focus:** helping people understand their options. Educational estimates—not professional tax, legal or investment advice.

### Stack City · Flight to a Million
A Unity game featuring a flying jet-wallet, wealth pickups, playful hazards, animated destinations and a race toward a million-dollar milestone. Development includes an endless-world system, challenge cards and automated gameplay tests.

**Focus:** responsive gameplay, personality and replayability.
